# Worldwide region / parish / peril evidence: non-admission preflight

**Date: 2026-11-01.** Research only. This document and its offline code
create no insurer policy, hazard, building geometry or catastrophe model.
In this context a **parish** is a local administrative unit (for example a
Louisiana county-equivalent); a **peril** is a natural catastrophe hazard such
as river flood, windstorm or earthquake. Both dimensions are essential and
must be separate keys.

## Existing usable pieces and exact boundaries

The production **CAT-only** Neon project `insurance-risk-core` (PostgreSQL
17/PostGIS, separate from Pokémon systems) currently holds 114 source
registry records. These are **15 admitted manifest identities** (14 strictly
metadata-only; one narrow ESRM20 approved-derived Kosovo taxonomy), 41
landscape source-shard receipts, and 58 documented/probe/research access
records. **Zero source records have `admissible_for_raw=true`**, and
production has **zero insured exposure units, zero physical events, zero
model runs**. The isolated validation branch has 12 wholly fictitious
exposure units and the existing synthetic 10,000-year SQL GUL test;
neither constitutes a real underwriting portfolio or native end-to-end CAT.

- **Geographic geometry:** geoBoundaries gbOpen original DEU/ADM1 was
  independently fetched and SHA-verified (6,254,151 bytes, 16 valid
  polygons) in public original-source workflow
  [41313687185](https://github.com/pokekarten/OpenCatastrophe-data/actions/runs/41313687185).
  That source is **2021 vintage**, not an automatically current/historical
  statutory crosswalk or insured exposure.
- **Buildings:** official Luxembourg ACT2026 geometry and EUBUCCO
  provenance investigations provide bounded geometric/source QA. The
  original ACT2026 230,353-feature survey included 66 invalid original
  geometries; raw geometry cannot be promoted without repair
  provenance, rights, spatial/temporal and scientific tests.
- **Wind:** a fixed original DWD station 00003 ZIP was hash-verified
  and 65,520 ten-minute gust observations (2010 to March 2011)
  quality-checked in
  [workflow 41316105378](https://github.com/pokekarten/OpenCatastrophe-data/actions/runs/41316105378).
  A single station's gusts are neither a regional windstorm event
  catalogue nor loss vulnerability.
- **Hydrology/flood:** PEGELONLINE / Hub'Eau gauges, historical CEMS
  activation/extent studies and GloFAS/JRC metadata can supply separate
  historical context, calibration and validation leads. A river gauge,
  satellite *estimated* flood mask, gridded return-period depth and
  measured insured claim are different quantities. Never infer depths or
  losses from spatial proximity alone.
- **Earthquake/TC/wildfire:** ESHM20/ESRM20, USGS, IBTrACS/STORM,
  NASA/CAL FIRE etc. have catalogue, metadata admission or bounded
  access/original-source checks. The hazard map or event track is not
  itself a site-resolved annual loss-generating model.
- **Insurer evidence:** EIOPA and US Florida/Texas aggregated research
  records exist but are neither named insurer street-address policies
  nor rights-cleared individual TIV and catastrophe treaty schedules.

These are **source-specific research successes**, not a global inventory
of all licensed/verified model-ready geographic and peril inputs.

  legal boundaries and names as of 2026-01-01, released 2026-10-14.

  Louisiana **parishes** are county equivalents; similarly different
  county-equivalent naming/structures exist elsewhere.

**Proven only:** authoritative catalogue, original archive filename,
release/vintage semantics and intended geographic unit type. **Not
proven yet:** downloaded original ZIP bytes and SHA-256; original

bytes; exact dataset reuse/license rights; all-border coverage versus
geoBoundaries ADM2; historical parish boundary reconciliation; any
insurance or CAT model input admission. No download or new DB import was
performed. Future requests must be newly reviewed/scoped and bounded
before fetching an ~80 MB original.

Do **not** equate the term parish with the hazard `peril`. A parish is
an ADM2-like local reporting unit. Peril-specific CAT event/footprint
scope and policy coverage apply *within* that geography.

## Bounded original 2026 Census COUNTY archive integrity runner

The official 2026 source catalogue and release date are not enough to
certify the archive's original polygon bytes. This PR also adds:

- `research_runners/us_census_2026_county_zip_audit_v0.py`: strictly
  local, standard-library inspection of the original national COUNTY
  ZIP. Supports one exact filename `tl_2026_us_county.zip` and only
  known Shapefile members with small optional Census XML metadata
  sidecars. Checks ZIP member safety, byte/expansion limits, member
  CRC via extraction, Shapefile Polygon and DBF headers, SHX/SHP
  index offsets, polygon record lengths/part/point layout and exact
  DBF row/index count, unique `STATEFP+COUNTYFP=GEOID`, original
  NAD83 geographic PRJ and observed Orleans Parish GEOID 22071.
- `tests/test_us_census_2026_county_zip_audit_v0.py`: fully fictitious
  tiny archives proving positive structure and adversarial mismatch
  rejection (invalid hash, bad index, missing geometry, unsafe ZIP
  member/path, malformed GEOID, bad CRS and more).
- `.github/workflows/us-census-2026-county-original-audit.yml`:
  on PRs, **offline synthetic tests only**. One exact ~80 MB
  original source download occurs only after the reviewed workflow
  file reaches `main` (or on a later explicitly manually dispatched
  run). The original ZIP is temporarily stored and removed, with
  a maximum 125 MB HTTP response, 210-second deadline, no retries,
  one official HTTPS host and no uploaded provider geometry.

The original ZIP's SHA-256 is **observed** by the validator unless
an independently established SHA-256 is explicitly supplied; the
original-authority hash is **not** guessed from catalogue text.
Only a separately witnessed second acquisition/pin can establish
repeatable original-byte identity. The original provider source
may fail when checked, and failures must remain fail-closed rather
than lowering the schema or adding ad-hoc unreviewed hosts.

The result is an **archive structure/source identity receipt only**.
It is not proof of complete US county polygon topology or all
parish boundaries, a licence to redistribute Census files,
a spatially resolved historical/event-year model, or a validated
OpenCat/NextGen country-to-parish exposure join. A later dedicated
topology/spatial completeness pass must use a reviewed GIS
backend and source vintage before modelling.

Run offline:

```sh
python -m unittest tests.test_us_census_2026_county_zip_audit_v0 -v
```

Inspect a *separately, lawfully obtained* original archive:

```sh
  --source-zip /private/tl_2026_us_county.zip
```

## Verified synthetic point-to-parish spatial crosswalk (2026-11-02)

The new `research_runners/us_census_2026_parish_point_crosswalk_v0.py`
makes the boundary research materially useful for a *future*
site-to-region model handoff without creating any fictional claim of
real insured exposure.

[Exact-source GitHub Actions run 41492945818](https://github.com/pokekarten/OpenCatastrophe-data/actions/runs/41492945818)
successfully retrieved the **83,378,454-byte** original 2026
Census national COUNTY ZIP, rechecked the independently pinned
SHA-256 `b527c9ba7aeb8f17ef118f026eae5e5a938aa1d00b873bc32342d0dce59321c0`,
revalidated all **64 Louisiana parish polygons**, and exercised
**40 passing** focused synthetically constructed source, geometry,
identity, and crosswalk test methods.

For every original Parish polygon, the runner creates a fresh,
obviously **invented synthetic point** using the *same polygon's*
`representative_point()`. It does **not** pretend that those 64
points are measured buildings, real claims/policies, or statistically
representative assets. Each point must land within exactly **one**
strict polygon interior, not on a boundary or in two polygons.
All **64/64** points matched unique parish GEOIDs on the genuine
source geometries, with none unassigned or ambiguous.

The output expresses the matching **as synthetic OpenCat
`RegionAssignment`-style fields** (only three illustrative rows
displayed; full list is deterministically hashed):

```json
{
  "asset_id": "synthetic_sample_22071",
  "country_code": "US",
  "subdivisions": ["US-LA", "US-LA-22071"]
}
```

The canonical **64-row synthetic assignment** hash is
`1f54b0a387831fc7a20ba801632284142be685f03b0823ab6d72df60d8e4b57f`.
The official geographic namespace is `US_CENSUS_TIGER_2026`,
source year **2026**, ISO2 `US` / ISO3 `USA`, and
the local official parish GEOID remains `22071`; the prefixed
`US-LA-22071` is a **derived model hierarchy label**, not a
different official Census identifier.

The public reproducibility scope includes 12 synthetic crosswalk
challenge methods rejecting wrong/hijacked asset IDs, non-finite
locations, other countries' IDs, missing sites, different-region
overlap, direct boundary points and nearest-parish fallbacks.

**Limits:** Representative points are chosen *from the polygons
being tested*, not independently observed exposure; this is a
mechanical geospatial integration smoke test, not an independent
verification of whether a human-addressed property is physically
located where advertised. This PR emits **no** source coordinates
or real asset information, no insured TIV, no claim, no hazard
intensity, no science/licensing approval and no native OpenCat/NextGen

## Versioned region x peril key and evidence preflight

`scripts/region_peril_readiness_v0.py` accepts one explicit, locally
supplied JSON document with a five-component geographic identifier
`ISO3:ADMIN_SYSTEM:ADM_LEVEL:OFFICIAL_ID:BOUNDARY_YEAR` and one peril.
Examples:

- `USA:US_CENSUS_TIGER_2026:ADM2:22071:2026` plus `flood_river`
  (five-digit Census county-equivalent GEOID; 22071 is Orleans Parish,
  Louisiana).
- For German federal states, a future example must use a verified
  **per-feature** geoBoundaries `shapeID` or official code at the
  source's reviewed year. `DEU-ADM1-10402087` identifies the whole
  2021 boundary **layer**, so it must **not** be used as an individual
  state's `admin_id`. A DEU ADM1 unit example remains deliberately
  unpopulated until the geometry/official-code crosswalk is checked.
- A country can use ADM0–5 where original administrative sublevels
  genuinely exist. Lack of a source-defined ADM4 is a documented gap,
  never an excuse to fabricate a polygon by joining names.

Every role-specific receipt must have the **same administrative system,
exact local code and vintage**. Hazard and policy evidence must additionally
have the **exact same peril**, rather than using all-peril metadata or
another hazard's calibrated vulnerability. General boundaries and
asset locators may explicitly use `peril=all`. Required roles:

1. `admin_boundary`
2. `asset_geometry`
3. `asset_values`
4. `hazard_event_catalogue`
5. `hazard_intensity`
6. `vulnerability`
7. `policy_terms`
8. `validation_holdout`

A catalogued source does not prove original bytes. A caller claiming a
SHA-256-checked original cannot itself prove hash authenticity, rights,
scientific suitability, vulnerability calibration, correlated world/event
IDs, insurance TIV, treaty terms or loss holdout independence. The tool
**always** returns
`automatic_model_admission_allowed=false` and
`rights_and_scientific_sufficiency_established=false`, even if eight
claimed receipts are supplied. It does not touch network, database or
provider files; it emits only evidence readiness/gaps.

Run locally from a trusted clean checkout:

```sh
python scripts/render_public_views.py --check --scope landscape

```

## New additive missing-cell matrix for regional and peril coverage

The single-cell preflight is intentionally not a global completeness claim.
`scripts/region_peril_coverage_matrix_v0.py` now produces an **explicit
cross-product** of exactly declared target administrative units and declared
perils. A cell with no supplied research evidence appears in the output with
all eight roles missing; it is **not** silently omitted or inferred from
neighboring regions or other perils.

Example: two separately code-identified Louisiana parish/county-equivalent
targets and two named perils produce four separate rows. If a research
catalogue receipt is supplied for one parish/flood pair, the other three
cells remain empty. A municipal flood receipt cannot automatically prove
another parish, another geographic vintage or another peril. These are
illustrative synthetic test scopes, not original source-geometry evidence.

Call the standalone tool with explicit, locally controlled JSON:

```json
{
  "schema_version": "OC_REGION_PERIL_MATRIX_V0",
  "regions": [
    {"country_iso3":"USA","admin_system":"US_CENSUS_TIGER_2026",
     "admin_level":"ADM2","admin_id":"22071","boundary_year":2026},
    {"country_iso3":"USA","admin_system":"US_CENSUS_TIGER_2026",
     "admin_level":"ADM2","admin_id":"22073","boundary_year":2026}
  ],
  "perils": ["flood_river","windstorm"],
  "evidence": []
}
```

Run:

```sh
python -m unittest tests.test_region_peril_coverage_matrix_v0 -v
python scripts/region_peril_coverage_matrix_v0.py /private/declaration.json
```

The output includes each cell's requested perimeter, missing role lists,
whether any evidence document was supplied, and whether original-source
QC was *claimed*. Such claims are **not independently verified**. The
matrix has strict bounded dimensions and disallows unknown cells,
duplicate targets, cross-peril evidence or self-proclaimed admission.
Even 8/8 claimed roles in all cells do **not** establish source rights,
geometry completeness, calibrated damage, observed insured portfolios,
reinsurance/retro or commercial model production.

The independent OpenCatastrophe [regional synthetic aggregation PR #16](https://github.com/pokekarten/OpenCatastrophe/pull/16) tackles a
**different** layer: hierarchy-aware reconciliation of source-generated
synthetic damage at explicit geographic keys. Do not confuse that
calculation with this public **source evidence** matrix; there is no
authorised native consumer of this data yet.

## Implementation ordering for worldwide CAT coverage

- **Geographic backbone:** freeze release/version, ISO3, ADM0–5/official
  local IDs, CRS, vintage, rights, source-level overlaps/gaps. Prefer
  official datasets and geoBoundaries gbOpen where country-specific
  licensing permits. Avoid unrestricted GADM use in commercial
  insurance modelling: its generic license is noncommercial.
- **Hazard per peril:** obtain original frequency and physical
  occurrence/footprint data *with a common year/event tape*; keep
  return-period maps separate from stochastic event sets and
  windstorm/hail/flood/earthquake/TC/wildfire/tsunami definitions.
- **Physical buildings:** link exact geolocated assets and vintage to
  the administrative units with geometric boundary policies and
  uncertainty. Buildings, households and insurance policies are
  different objects.
- **Insurer-specific terms:** hold observed insurer exposure/TIV,
  deductibles, limits, shares, peril coverage, Cat XL/reinstatements,
  retrocession and accounting currency separately from global hazard.
  Never disaggregate aggregated market rows to invented properties.
- **Evidence/validation:** independent observed event/damage holdouts,
  spatial and temporal generalisation tests, source reuse/lineage checks,
  rights and redistribution reviews, reproducible native NextGen/Oasis/
  OpenQuake execution before any production CAT model version.

**No global/peril model completeness claim follows** from a candidate
register, catalogued national boundaries or simulated 10k toy years.
