# Initial carrier-specific synthetic CAT portfolio pilots: primary-source comparison (2026-11-04)

**Research-only selection memo. Not an admission of provider data, insured TIV, actual policy location, catastrophe-model output, licensing rights or a production-ready insured portfolio.** This is a dated selection and experimental-design rationale; canonical source identities and approvals remain in `landscape/`, `manifests/` and `docs/source-reviews/`. No source bytes or individual policy records are copied here.

## Decision: focus on property coverage rather than composite insurers or reinsurers

The best identifiable **first** portfolio class is a **geographically bounded, direct residential/commercial property book** where insurer/programme-level policy counts, location-specific insured exposure, coverage definition, written premiums, event hazard and independent aggregate loss benchmarks overlap. A global multinational group / treaty reinsurer has good financial reporting but very weak direct insured-site identifiability. Separate **observed constraints**, **synthetic priors**, **holdouts**, **inferred contract assumptions** and **measured/modelled losses**.

Selection criteria (qualitative; *not* a measured fit or official ranking): (1) unambiguous insurance perimeter and coverage; (2) dated insurance counts and financial denominators; (3) fine enough **observed** company geography; (4) physical-building/valuation priors; (5) open hazard and vulnerability pathway; (6) truly independent AAL/PML and gross/net targets; (7) provider rights/access and original-byte reproducibility. One criterion cannot substitute for another.

## Source evidence verified or discovered in this research pass

### TWIA: strongest exact year-end policy + external CAT validation combination

- [TWIA 2025-12-31 quarterly liability / statistical report](https://www.twia.org/wp-content/uploads/TWIA-Liability-Report-20251231.pdf): reported **284,846** policies in force; **USD 126,455,724,600** exposure in force; **USD 820,864,531** 2025 YTD written premium, with detailed county rows. Explicitly freeze exact PDF version/bytes and distinguish exposure from TIV/policy limits in other publications.
- [TWIA 2025 rate adequacy source product](https://www.twia.org/about-us/financial-reports/2025-rate-adequacy-analysis/): county AAL results from Verisk/RMS/CoreLogic/Impact Forecasting; vendor model *event file* links; frequency and assumptions. These are **holdouts**, not freely assumed calibration/training inputs. This rate study refers to different catastrophe-model in-force assumptions (see [2025 model assumptions](https://www.twia.org/wp-content/uploads/Assumptions-2025.pdf)).
- [TWIA 2026 rate adequacy source product](https://www.twia.org/about-us/financial-reports/2026-rate-adequacy-analysis/): additional independent reporting vintage with county AAL/vendor/event outputs; requires exact-version/term check. A same-report storm catalogue is not an independent validation sample if it is also used to fit the model.
- [TWIA coverage and eligible counties](https://www.twia.org/coverage-eligibility/): wind/hail and limited coastal territory; do not treat as all-peril property exposure. Need 2025 storm/claims and 2025-11-30 model-exposure apples-to-apples comparison.

### Citizens: primary county *and* coverage scope

- [Citizens 2025-12-31 exposure by product](https://www.citizensfla.com/-/20251231-policies-in-force): **395,337** policies and **USD 128,482,188,755** published total exposure; separate personal/commercial wind-only versus multiperil and premiums.
- [Citizens 2025-12-31 exact county report](https://www.citizensfla.com/documents/20702/30188297/20251231+Detail+by+County.pdf): observed **county x policy-type**, PIF, building count, premiums and total exposure, excluding takeouts; validate full county-total and product-total reconciliation before model preparation.
- [Citizens 2025-12-31 corporate analytics report](https://www.citizensfla.com/documents/20702/93064/20251231+Business+Overview.pdf/6345624f-a7a1-94cc-80d3-b41780887d9d?t=1770040409863): county-concentration, product/exposure definitions and catastrophe AAL benchmarking; distinguish official vendor output from an independent open model.
- [Florida OIR Market Intelligence Reporting](https://qsrng.floir.gov/Reports/ReportCriteriaWizard.aspx) and [official source/granularity explanation](https://floir.gov/tools-and-data): monthly as of 2025, carrier-level by county and ZIP when the exact query/download supports it; **self-reported/unaudited** and subject to revision. The repository already has a *different* statewide 442-row staged Florida extract, not automatically this detailed Citizens dataset.

### Switzerland: clearer insured-universe perimeter

- [GVZ 2025 figures](https://www.gvz.ch/hauptnavigation/gvz-allgemein/zahlen-und-fakten): **303,131** insured buildings and **CHF 642.5bn** insured capital; separate premiums/claims. [GVZ annual report](https://www.gvz.ch/hauptnavigation/gvz-allgemein/geschaeftsberichte) and [peril/mandatory insurance definition](https://www.gvz.ch/hauptnavigation/gvz-allgemein) describe insurer perimeter. Earthquake is limited, not assumed equivalent to mandatory elemental cover.
- [GVB 2025 annual-reports landing](https://gvb.ch/de/gvb-gruppe/portraet/geschaeftsberichte.html) and [2025 original financial report](https://gvb.ch/content/dam/gini/geschaeftsbericht/2025/gvb-jahresrechnung-2025.pdf): original report states 408,321 insured canton buildings and CHF 471.0bn indexed insured building value; full perimeter/vintage must be checked against core GVB versus consolidated subsidiaries and index changes.
- [Swiss official GWR building-status service](https://opendata.swiss/de/dataset/eidg-gebaude-und-wohnungsregister-gebaudestatus), [Swiss building statistics](https://opendata.swiss/de/dataset/gebaude-und-wohnungsstatistik-gebaude). These are physical stock/attribute priors **not** the carrier's internal location/TIV file; GWR/AV joining must be independently reviewed. [Swiss AG example of GWR-enriched geometries](https://opendata.swiss/de/dataset/gebaudeflachen-mit-informationen-der-eidg-gebaude-und-wohnungsregisters) is *Aargau*, not Zürich/Bern: do not use it as if it covered either pilot canton.

### California wildfire: high-quality reported risk geography

- [California FAIR Plan official statistics and downloads](https://www.cfpnet.com/key-statistics-data/): **2026-09** fiscal-year-end **698,307** policies and **USD 773bn** exposure (reported). Download/product links include county/ZIP, residential and commercial, policy counts/premium/exposure and wildfire risk score. Explicitly label the 2026-09 vintage, not 2025.
- Treat FAIR Plan exposure as basic-property coverage for a residual market, not representative of all California buildings and not independently equivalent to replacement cost, fire-only peril liability or total statewide insurance coverage.

### Germany and flood anchor already researched in the repository

- [Allianz Germany standalone reports through FY2025](https://www.allianz.de/unternehmen/zahlen-daten-fakten/geschaeftsberichte/). Separate the *Allianz Versicherungs-AG* from Allianz Deutschland parent/group. [Independent German market summary, 2026-10-26](https://www.versicherungsjournal.de/markt-und-politik/die-groessten-wohngebaeudeversicherer-157000) reports Allianz more than 2.3m residential-building policies as of 2025; primary report is the authoritative next extraction target.
- [Existing German Zensus/insurer bridge](zensus-gdv-2022-2025-insurer-district-bridge-20261103.md) explicitly addresses suppressed regional census cells and GDV interactive-map text/data-mining restrictions; no permission for bulk GDV scraping is implied.
- [Existing NFIP v3 aggregate preflight](../NFIP_V3_AGGREGATE_PREFLIGHT_V0.md): correct modern API names `NfipPolicies`/`NfipClaims`; source rights/data sensitivity constraints; previous v2 source is deprecated. Do not introduce v2 fallback.
- [Existing Florida/Texas exposure QA](florida-texas-exposure-reconciliation-v0.md): **staged statewide company and Texas ZIP market stock are different source populations**. Do not mistake these views for TWIA or Citizens exact county exposures.
- [Existing portfolio identifiability principles](insurer-portfolio-reverse-engineering-evidence-20261103.md): premium marginals do not identify insurer-location TIV, risk-bearing roles and contract graphs, holdout vs fitting, legal perimeter and synthetic uncertainty.

## Executable pilot acceptance contract to implement *after* source rights and original-byte receipts

1. **Freeze source product and exactly matched vintage**: carrier/programme ID, policy/peril scope, counts, total exposure definition, year-end/reinsurance-vintage, county/ZIP group, reporting currency, official URL, original-response SHA-256, row schema, legal permission and separate model-science approval.
2. **Expose an observed aggregate-only table** with `region_id, coverage_class, policy_count, reported_exposure, premium, as_of, reported_measure_type`; never infer individual insurance customers from a public building footprint.
3. **Generate two or more independent synthetic location/allocation scenarios** from independently sourced physical-building stock, valuations, construction/age/occupancy and hazard priors, each matching carrier aggregate constraints. Exclude a source row on missing/suppressed values instead of filling zero. Preserve full roster including zero-loss assets.
4. **Run one explicit peril and event-vintage** (e.g. TWIA wind excluding surge) with admitted hazard, vulnerability and scientific data split; output OED, GUL, IL, event loss, annual loss, AAL, OEP/AEP and, if recorded, a separately labelled scenario contract tower for net loss. Use independent events/years and calibrated frequency, **never vendor PML as an input and then validation against the same target**.
5. **Independent checks**: observed county x coverage totals and full-year consistency; sum of exposure values; event/annual loss conservation; for non-negative yearly losses under a matched definition, **AEP loss >= OEP loss** at the same exceedance probability; loss ordering; return-period, gross-vs-net, currency/unit, period-vintage and uncertainty audit; archive configuration, input hashes, code HEAD, RNG seed and status.
6. **No-go until** model-input admission, physical-model validity and loss-distribution validation are established separately. Public 2025 financials and underwriting-level AAL cannot themselves prove insurer-specific property-level vulnerability, event frequency or private reinsurance tower.

## Suggested order, with explicit separate goals

- **Most convincing first Oasis wind end-to-end demonstrator:** TWIA 2025-12-31 county/coverage portfolio and **independent** 2025/26 vendor-report holdouts after date/scope reconciliation. Citizens is the next U.S. property carrier to stress-test product splits and depopulation.
- **Cleanest insured building-population identity problem:** GVZ Zürich 2025, then GVB Bern, with GWR/canton building-geometry crosswalk and mandatory cover semantics.
- **First wildfire:** California FAIR Plan 2026-09 ZIP x policy-category x wildfire-risk-score data, after data-rights and open hazard/vulnerability checks.
- **First private multinational/group-adjacent carrier:** Allianz Versicherungs-AG **solo**, initially residential building only, two nonidentical geographic priors rather than pretending a known insurer county allocation.
- **Flood programme external validation:** FEMA NFIP **v3 only** with aggregate-only privacy compliance, not a normal private-company insured building roster.
