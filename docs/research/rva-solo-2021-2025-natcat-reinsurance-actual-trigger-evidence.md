# R+V Allgemeine 2021–2025: actual **outward NatCat reinsurance** legal and historical evidence

**Source review 2026-11-04, research only.** Unlike an invented treaty tower or the R+V parent group's annual reinsurer portfolio, this note uses the **R+V Allgemeine Versicherung AG solo** direct-business passages of the primary HGB financial reports. The machine-readable issuer evidence is [rva_solo_public_natcat_ri_2021_2025_v0.toml](../../research/observations/rva_solo_public_natcat_ri_2021_2025_v0.toml), with ten offline typed negative/positive tests. No original primary PDFs have been byte-hashed in this new pass and no proprietary treaty wordings are available.

## What *is* actually established

The annual reports **2021, 2023, 2024 and 2025** describe **quota-share and excess-of-loss reinsurance** for the company's directly written business, with an **additional NatCat excess-of-loss treaty** placed with the related, distinct legal entity **R+V Versicherung AG**. That parent company in turn retrocedes the described NatCat large-loss protection to outside reinsurers. The **2022 report** explicitly mentions excess of loss and the additional group NatCat treaty but does **not** mention quota share in that same paragraph: this is unknown information, **not proof no quota existed in 2022**.

**CRITICAL numeric accounting boundary:** The **signed annual technical result** of the reinsurance ceded by the direct book, e.g. **+€470.6m in 2021**, is **NOT** a measured Bernd event reinsurance recovery, **not** a stand-alone Cat-XL payout, **not** the treaty premium and not the difference between one observed gross/net CAT loss. The result spans many perils, events, premium payments, claims and reserve development, potentially multiple treaties and inuring layers. Negative 2022–2025 values are not proof reinsurance delivered zero ceded claims. Conversely, the **specific parent NatCat *large-event* protection** was explicitly reported untriggered in those four years, but that does not mean other risk/claims excess covers were not used.

The 2021 NatCat protection **trigger** is a rare actual observed Boolean control for our stress-test designs. However five annual Booleans cannot identify monetary **attachment**, **limit**, **quota**, number of reinstatements, per-occurrence vs aggregate cover, inuring order, reinstatement premium, geographical perimeter or external retro proportions; these must remain genuinely **hypothetical** and cannot be reverse-solved from group Bernd €730m press estimate. Even 2021 event chronology is not one single Bernd hazard event: other June/July storms may have aggregated into the contractual result.

## Newly recovered actual SOLO gross catastrophe benchmark and building-claims history

The **R+V Allgemeine 2022 HGB** [official report, PDF pp. 9–10](https://www.ruv.de/dam/jcr:55c81597-b4fe-4df2-a3e0-65aedb9ba15a/2022-allgemeine.pdf) retrospectively reports **€949.8m of 2021 gross *current-year catastrophe/natural elemental claim expense*** in this solo entity. The source explicitly connects the historic severe 2021 season to the June–July storms including **Volker, Wolfgang, Xero and Bernd**. This is much stronger than any *group* Bernd press snapshot as a **company-level annual gross NatCat severity holdout**. **It is not a single Bernd event loss**, not a connected-buildings-only loss, and not the 2021 parent NatCat XL cession or standalone premium. Do not subtract the 2021 €470.6m *ceded annual total technical result* from €949.8m and label the difference as model company net CAT: the two account on different perils, contract cashflows, reserve development, and financial signs.

## Independent parent/inward context and name risk

R+V Versicherung AG is a **different** company acting as parent/internal reinsurer, while R+V Allgemeine's direct residential/commercial policies are issued by the **solo direct insurer**. The parent insurer/reinsurer annual [2025 report](https://www.rv-re.com/about-us/ratings-reports) covers far more worldwide inward reinsurance business and should NEVER be used as the target's actual building-risk geography or premium numerator. The R+V Allgemeine separately reports a modest **inward** book (2025 gross assumed business premium approximately €98.6m): do NOT mix with its **outward** signed technical-result control.

Corporate year-on-year changes from Condor (2024) and R+V Direktversicherung (2025) additionally affect premium and contract perimeters. Yearly technical result is **not** normalized to unchanged treaty/portfolio terms.

## What we can now build correctly in OpenCatastrophe/NextGen/Oasis

For a purely **synthetic** R+V-like book, distinguish:
1. **Gross ground-up** event loss by fictional location, structural class and one licensed calibrated Germany peril event surface; aggregate by consistent period/event IDs.
2. **Insurance gross after policy financial conditions** (retentions, limits, explicit natural-peril cover) at location/account/policy, wholly hypothetical until issuer policy-distribution data exists.
3. **Hypothetical primary proportional reinsurance (QS)** and working surplus/XL, but do not assume the named quota/share applies to the whole book or is uniform across LoB. The 2022 source wording differs.
4. **A hypothetical group ceded NatCat XL to parent R+V Versicherung AG**; acknowledge real *treaty class and counterparty* observed in 2021–2025, but **all monetary terms invented**. Do not split group insured losses incorrectly into issuer event payouts.
5. **Separate parent external retrocession** as a distinct *parent* accounting/contract graph, **not** a direct issuer net layer applied a second time to the same loss.
6. Produce native same-world GUL → IL → outward ceded → issuer net annual OEP/AEP/AAL; challenge 2021 as a historical **qualitative Boolean** expected-trigger study and other years as absent-trigger reports **only after** matched hazard, portfolio vintage and whole-cat per-year simulations. No actual actuarial annual probabilities/1:200 PML from these source notes.

The exact source passages can be located under original report headings **“In Rückdeckung gegebenes Versicherungsgeschäft”** and **“Versicherungsleistungen”**. R+V Allgemeine 2024 PDF printed pp. 10–12 especially discloses supplemental internal NatCat treaty and external retro. Internal net CAT **cannot be calculated from these limited disclosures**. If correct CAT model calibration is the goal, the high-value missing source classes are actual insured-property regional aggregates, the treaty schedule (retention/attachment/exhaustion/limits/inuring/reinstatements), insurer original event-level paid+reserved gross and ceded claims, precise policy endorsement mix, and a scientifically versioned independent hazard/vulnerability model.
