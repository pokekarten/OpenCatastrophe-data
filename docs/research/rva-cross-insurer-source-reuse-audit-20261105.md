# Reusing the R+V 2025 source research in cross-insurer portfolio reports

**Review checkpoint: 2026-11-05. Research-only index; this document is not a new dataset, source admission, licence clearance, model calibration or insurer exposure observation.** The goal is to prevent valuable source findings from remaining discoverable only by searching for R+V. This file points to the existing *canonical* research observations; it must not duplicate their typed figures as a second source of truth.

## Registry and evidence separation

* `landscape/sources*.json`: canonical **generic discovery candidates**, with generated Markdown projections; a candidate is **not admitted**. For German generic insurer risk evidence, start at [German carrier exposure priors](../../landscape/sources-german-carrier-exposure-priors-20261103.json), [portfolio reverse-engineering](../../landscape/sources-portfolio-reverse-engineering-20261103.json), [insurance market calibration](../../landscape/sources-insurance-market-calibration.json) and [global insurer filings](../../landscape/sources-global-insurer-2025-filings-20261103.json).
* `research/observations/*.toml`: **source-linked company-specific observations and research candidates**. These are not necessarily eligible model inputs. R+V material includes [2019–2025 solo HGB](../../research/observations/rva_solo_hgb_property_2019_2025_v0.toml), [external candidates](../../research/observations/rva_external_calibration_source_candidates_v0.toml), [2025 German geography](rva-2025-issuer-anchored-geography-identification-v0.md) and [new distributor/procurement discoveries](../../research/observations/rva_distribution_bancassurance_public_awards_20261105_v0.toml).
* `manifests/` and `docs/source-reviews/`: independently reviewed admissions; **none of the links here is newly admitted by this index**. Do not upload third-party original PDFs, personal agency contact/address lists or insurer-customer asset schedules to the public repo.

## Report-ready method for any insurer

For each insurer legal entity and reference year, maintain **four independent evidence columns**:

1. **Observed company controls:** legal entity, HGB/QRT product class, exact reporting perimeter, contract-count denominator, gross/net accounting measure, vintage and original URL.
2. **Observed named-account relationships:** original corporate procurement / parliamentary / annual-report attestation; relationship start/end or unknown, carrier vs consortium, confidence, explicit negative controls.
3. **Observed physical opportunity:** independently published owner/group dwellings, building counts, areas, PV units, agriculture, land use and hazard at a declared geography/date. **Do not** label these as covered risks.
4. **Inferred synthetic allocation:** separate scenario ID and uncertainty range for selection/take-up, site-to-account assignment, valuations, peril coverage and co-insurance. Never convert a synthetic case into an observed carrier fact.

**Critical physical/financial separation:** Source discovery → source rights → reproducible bytes and schema → scientific fitness → admitted model input → loss-model run → gross/insured/net OEP/AEP. No links above grant the downstream stages. This research contains **no verified R+V insured address/TIV schedule, actual outward treaty structure, calibrated Germany CAT event/loss model or insurer OEP/AEP**.
