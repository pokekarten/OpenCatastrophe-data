# R+V: external CAT geography, frequency, valuation and independent benchmark evidence (2026-11-04)

**Status: new source discovery and independent portfolio-calibration design ONLY.** Target: **R+V Allgemeine Versicherung AG (solo direct insurer)**, not its parent R+V Versicherung AG, the R+V group, KRAVAG, VTV, Assímoco or an assumed reinsurance tower. This complements the 2019–2025 HGB history, fictional R+V bridge and the contemporaneous [R+V policy/tariff claims PR #1311](https://github.com/pokekarten/OpenCatastrophe-data/pull/1311); it does **not** copy or overwrite its source rows. **21 separately classified source candidates** are in [strict JSON research register](../../research/observations/rva_external_calibration_source_candidates_v0.json). No downloaded original assets, third-party bulk data, confidential policyholder information, GEOCODED R+V locations, model runs or DB writes.

## New checks that change the actual portfolio design

1. **Observed legal-entity line/accounting controls, not a GIS/TIV oracle.** The [official R+V annual-report portal](https://www.ruv.de/ueber-uns/unternehmen/geschaeftsberichte) and [2025 HGB original](https://www.ruv.de/dam/jcr:e0e32e31-09ff-4b0f-b693-2c6c9bee9bb5/01_GB%202025_R+V%20Allgemeine%20Versicherung%20AG_final.2026-04-21-14-59-23.pdf) disclose **2025 HGB self-written direct premium and gross current-underwriting-year claims** (each EUR million, rounded in printed tables):

   | Solo HGB direct class | 2025 booked GWP, €m | 2025 gross current-year claim expense, €m | 2024 gross current-year claim expense, €m |
   | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
   | Verbundene Gebäude | 997.710 | 601.698 | 696.566 |
   | Verbundene Hausrat | 164.585 | 75.282 | 92.008 |
   | Feuer | 305.990 | 178.613 | 221.852 |
   | Sonstige Sach | 657.188 | 405.037 | 446.781 |

   These are **all-cause, class-level financial controls**, not CAT occurrence losses, not earned premium loss ratios, not paid cash, and not after reinsurance. The HGB sum of premiums is €2,125.473m; the Solvency-II Fire/Other Property €2,223.182m is **different scope**, not a data-error reconciliation. For actual 2025 contract counts and the 2024/25 accounting detail, see #1311. **Never use reported 2025 P&C total or the gross all-cause loss to calibrate a specific wind/flood/hail vulnerability function.**

2. **Separate insurer and group event observations.** [Bernd event 2021](https://www.ruv.de/newsroom/pressemitteilungen/2022-07-07-420-millionen-euro-an-flutopfer-gezahlt): the 2022-07 retrospective reports about 15,000 claims, €730m losses and 600 large losses amounting to €450m **for R+V customers** (not proven R+V Allgemeine solo; estimates can develop). [2024 June BY/BW flooding](https://www.ruv.de/newsroom/pressemitteilungen/2024-06-06-hochwasser-schaeden-juni) was an **early** ~€100m R+V loss estimate, with many building claims; not a final claim triangle. [2024 hail](https://www.ruv.de/newsroom/pressemitteilungen/2025-05-21-hagelschaeden): ~€97m all R+V classes includes ~€56m **motor**; the residual is not necessarily *only residential-building* hail loss. [2025 hail](https://www.ruv.de/newsroom/pressemitteilungen/2026-05-29-schaeden-hagel-2025): ~€56m total, motor majority. [2025 group elemental results](https://www.ruv.de/newsroom/pressemitteilungen/2026-04-13-bilanzpressekonferenz): €150m (2024 €442m), **group** not solo. [2025 water building claim distribution](https://www.ruv.de/newsroom/pressemitteilungen/2026-03-12-leitungswasser-schaden-haus): 45,400 water claims; reported 61% water / 29% weather and flood / 10% fire among reported building incidents. No verified solo denominator; do not extrapolate 29% to R+V Allgemeine contract population or treat 45,400 as CAT claims. Use these group facts as **qualitative/tail challenge** only.

3. **Physical buildings independent from distribution presence.** [Destatis Zensus 2022 municipal/district building and dwelling tables](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Themen/Gesellschaft-Umwelt/Bevoelkerung/Zensus2022/Publikationen/publikationen-akkordeon-regionaltabellen.html) provide an observed **2022 physical stock**, subject to suppressed cells, vintage and residential-vs-commercial definitions. [R+V's group channel](https://www.ruv.de/ueber-uns/unternehmen/konzernstruktur) reports more than 640 partner cooperative banks and approximately 260 general agencies. Independent [BVR regional sector statistics 2025](https://berichte.bvr.de/jahresbericht-2025/ergebnisse/regionale-statistik/) cover **646 member institutions and 6,167 branches across cooperative sector categories** — NOT the R+V-partner bank roster, NOT 6,167 insurer offices, and **regional associations are not a national 16-state insured policy matrix**. Do not use BVR deposits/branch counts to fix uninsured insurer geography; they provide a distinct **marketing-channel sensitivity prior** only.

4. **Replacement costs: use two distinct index families.** Destatis [GENESIS 61261-0013](https://genesis.destatis.de/datenbank/online/statistic/61261/table/61261-0013/search/s/cHJlaXNl) is **residential building cost index** (2021=100; 2025 value 121.5 in currently published table) and differs from [GENESIS 61261-0001](https://genesis.destatis.de/datenbank/online/statistic/61261/details) **building price indices by residential/office/commercial category**, work component, VAT basis and vintage. Neither provides square-metre reinstatement cost or R+V sum insured; no conversion from GWP to TIV. If cost reconstruction is attempted, require measured/assumed floor area, use class, storeys, age, contents, BI, inflation year and coverage separately.

5. **Real DWD event candidate — explicit shared-source dependence.** DWD [CatRaRE W3 v2026.01](https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/help/landing_pages/doi_landingpage_CatRaRE_W3_Eta_v2026.01-de.html) and [T5 v2026.01](https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/help/landing_pages/doi_landingpage_CatRaRE_T5_Eta_v2026.01-de.html) offer nationwide **2001–2025** radar-rainfall event catalogues as CSV and FileGDB (with event dates/durations/maxima, polygons). Both rely on a **common RADKLIM-RW source**, so W3 and T5 are not two independent annual occurrence processes and should never be summed as independent Poisson streams. Preserve original event IDs and deduplicate multi-duration representations according to publisher semantics. [KOSTRA-DWD 2020](https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/help/landing_pages/doi_landingpage_KOSTRA_DWD_2020-de.html) gives rainfall **return-period marginals**, NOT historic event losses, spatial dependencies or flood depth. These data are meteorological drivers, *not* flood water-surface models.

6. **BKG pluvial-depth hazard: incomplete state coverage and modelling assumptions.** [Official BKG WMS and open-download portal](https://gdz.bkg.bund.de/index.php/default/open-data/wms-hinweiskarte-starkregengefahren-wms-starkregen.html) describes 1m simulation depths, runoff directions and velocities for heavy-rain scenarios. Its 2026 coverage is **not yet complete in Bayern, Baden-Württemberg, Hessen and Saarland**, especially critical for the 2024 R+V southern flood. BKG explicitly notes **infiltration and drainage/sewer system were neglected**; an apparent inundation is not a calibrated insured ground-floor depth and a missing region is UNKNOWN, not zero risk. The provider publishes attribution terms under DL-DE/BY-2.0 and a disclaimer; review individual tile/download reuse and vintage first.

7. **Industry insured loss independent in *population*, not entirely in events.** The [GDV Naturgefahrenreport 2026 data service](https://www.gdv.de/gdv/statistik/datenservice-zum-naturgefahrenreport) includes storms/hail, additional elementals, buildings/contents/commercial/motor, some state and multi-year rates, with a dedicated [state-level residential storm/hail time series since 1997](https://www.gdv.de/gdv/statistik/datenservice-zum-naturgefahrenreport/sachversicherung-sturm-hagel/schaeden-durch-sturm-und-hagel-an-wohngebaeuden-in-den-bundeslaendern-sehr-unterschiedlich--139176). **GDV [usage terms](https://www.gdv.de/gdv/statistik/nutzungsbedingungen-fuer-statistikdaten-und-deren-visualisierung-188704) reserve text/data mining and require explicit consent for TDM.** NO automated scrape, third-party raw row ingestion, model training or downstream redistribution is approved here. Only manual bibliographic source identification/limited fact review; seek documented permission for programmatic model calibration. An insurer contributes to industry statistics, so an industry series is not fully independent of the insurer loss used to calibrate on identical events.

## Concrete exposure-generation experiments (all scenario hypotheses)

Hold separately: **N** = observed insurer coverage contract count (not number of buildings); **B_r** = observed 2022 building opportunity per region, **M_r** = 2025 cooperative-bank distribution channel counts with uncertain relationship to insured take-up; **C_r** = risk construction/age/value proxies; **H_{r,p,e}** = event hazard/peril/vintage; **L** = policy financial terms and **R** = outward assumed reinsurance. No current source determines R+V's true county-to-site or risk count matrix. For the first model, simulate at least **three** geospatial hypotheses: **A** building-stock weighted, **B** building-type/urban-rural + construction-cost weighted, **C** *independently varied* channel-weight sensitivity. Normalize each to the same issuer-compatible **N** and deliberately vary concentration. Never simply set **R+V county policies = cooperative bank county members** or infer TIV from premium.

For **Wind**, seek DWD storms/gusts and one licensed, Germany-compatible stochastic track/windfield/vulnerability model with measured event-specific claims *not used during calibration*. For **Flood**, separate **fluvial vs pluvial**, do not multiply independent rainfall event counts by correlated BKG marginal scenario maps. First validate historical real events and historical building vintage. Build OED accounts, sites and explicitly **fictional** cover and outward QS/CAT-XL, verify identical event IDs across GUL→IL→RI→annual YLT, then AAL/OEP/AEP gross and net. Do not use 2025 group €150m as the solo model's annual catastrophe AAL or use a reported SCR stress as a PML.
