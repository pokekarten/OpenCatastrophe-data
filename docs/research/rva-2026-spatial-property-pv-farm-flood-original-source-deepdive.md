# R+V: 2026 original-source deep dive — spatial building risk, agriculture, PV, flood and historical loss evidence

**Researched 2026-11-04. Target legal entity: R+V Allgemeine Versicherung AG; not the R+V Versicherung AG group, its parent reinsurance, KRAVAG or Vereinigte Tierversicherung (VTV).** Public provider *candidates*, their precise roles, original URL, vintages, and restrictions are in [typed source receipt](../../research/observations/rva_spatial_risk_source_deepdive_20261104_v0.toml). All **28 entries are discovery receipts**; no underlying CSV, raster, XYZ building footprints, station data, official GML, insurer sites, user information, or complete XML is ingested, original-byte SHA obtained, rights accepted, calibrated hazard model or insurer portfolio inferred in this change. Existing [issuer HGB history](rva-solo-historical-property-sources-20261104.md), [R+V contracts](rva-group-claims-tariff-territory-triangulation-20261104.md), [NatCat RI proof](rva-solo-2021-2025-natcat-reinsurance-actual-trigger-evidence.md) and [external candidate registry](rva-external-calibration-spatial-hazard-priors-20261104.md) remain authoritative for their respective distinct measures. Do not duplicate their accounting observations.

## Main new independent physical-property denominators

**2026 Destatis Census specialised residential building products.** The publisher's [Zensus 2022 open grid portal](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Themen/Gesellschaft-Umwelt/Bevoelkerung/Zensus2022/_publikationen.html) provides open GIS ZIPs at 100m/1km/10km with declared reuse **with attribution** and separate description spreadsheets. Newly discovered [2026-02-26 codebook for heated buildings by age decade, building type/size and heating fuel at 1km](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Themen/Gesellschaft-Umwelt/Bevoelkerung/Zensus2022/Publikationen/Downloads-Publikationen/Sonderauswertungen/datensatzbeschreibung_gebaeude_nach_baujahr_typ_energie.html) and [2026-02-26 separate ownership-by-building 100m description](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Themen/Gesellschaft-Umwelt/Bevoelkerung/Zensus2022/Publikationen/Downloads-Publikationen/Sonderauswertungen/datensatzbeschreibung_gebaeude_nach_eigentumsform.html?templateQueryString=Zensus) add **physical building class/age** and *ownership* potential far beyond anonymous residential counts. They describe specific products; retrieve and separately hash the actual ZIPs + codebooks before claiming actual field/value content. Check small-cell suppression, nested field code taxonomy and cross-year Zensus/issuer mismatch. Heating type is no direct weather vulnerability, and ownership is not insurance policy take-up.

**Open original *state* 3D roofs are often better first pilot inputs than restricted national delivery.** [Bavaria LoD2](https://geodaten.bayern.de/opengeodata/OpenDataDetail.html?pn=lod2) offers **2×2 km, EPSG:25832, weekly updates, CityGML under CC BY 4.0**. [NRW original LoD2 open download](https://www.opengeodata.nrw.de/produkte/geobasis/3dg/lod2_gml/), [Saxony original 2×2 km tile portal](https://www.geodaten.sachsen.de/downloadbereich-digitale-3d-stadtmodelle-4875.html), and [Baden-Württemberg official LoD2](https://www.lglbw.de/Produkte/3D-Produkte/3D-Gebaeudemodelle/LoD2/) are candidate repeatable regional pilots. Attribute meaning: footprint/volume, estimated roof shapes and nominal heights. A roof shape alone says nothing about actual building roof material, unreported roof repairs, construction quality, 2021/2024 historical presence or *insurer* value. Distinguish 2026 3D geometries from historical peril-event year; avoid CRS errors for UTM32 vs UTM33. Baden-Württemberg's official roof form autoassignment is around 90% accurate, so preserve uncertain roof types.

**CRITICAL rights negative control:** [central official nationwide HU-DE](https://www.lvg.bayern.de/vermessung/zshh/hu-de.html) indicates roughly **59 million** official polygons and fees **from €4,000**, while [central BKG LoD2-DE](https://gdz.bkg.bund.de/index.php/default/3d-gebaudemodelle-lod2-deutschland-lod2-de.html) expressly limits to eligible user groups and licensed use. A central metadata page or free **test ZIP** does *not* make the 59m national object archive or BKG restricted CityGML a free public input. **Separate licences per state and central publisher**; maintain exact data-product ID and spatial release vintage to prevent combining inconsistent releases.

## Unexpected route to building contents and auxiliary assets

**Rooftop PV:** [NRW official Solarkataster publisher asset directory](https://www.opengeodata.nrw.de/produkte/umwelt_klima/energie/solarkataster/photovoltaik/) declares **DL-DE/Zero-2.0** and separately lists a **1.4MB March-2026 municipality × building-category PV potential Excel**, huge regional EPSG:25832 GeoDatabases, and municipal ZIP shapefiles. This supports a *roof geometry/technical PV potential* exposure proxy. **Potential is NOT actual installed equipment, contract endorsement rate or observed insured claim.**

**Independently measured-ish installed PV register:** Bundesnetzagentur's [official MaStR public full XML download](https://www.marktstammdatenregister.de/MaStR/Datendownload) says it is updated daily, supports dated snapshots, publishes the data under **Datenlizenz Deutschland – Namensnennung 2.0**, and revised its schema in October 2025. The November 2026 full XML is around **3.2 GB compressed**. This is a distinct registered generating-unit universe that can be **privacy-safely aggregated by district/commissioning year** after original schema/source-terms review; do NOT expose private owner or point address and do NOT join directly to insurer properties or treat rooftop potential as installed PV. Consider the publisher's [filtered public unit overview](https://www.marktstammdatenregister.de/MaStR/Einheit/Einheiten/ErweiterteOeffentlicheEinheitenuebersicht) before a multi-GB original ingest. PV installation information can be a risk modifier (hail panel loss, wind attachment, fire), not a substitute for insured building count.

**Replacement-cost archetypes:** [ImmoWertV Annex 4, NHK 2010](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/immowertv_2022/anlage_4.html) is the official legal definition of **normal construction costs by building type, standard and gross floor area (2010 EUR/m² BGF)** for single/multifamily homes, offices, mixed-use buildings, warehouses, workshops and many other types. This is materially more granular than simply multiplying a national construction index by a fictional TIV. The NHK are **valuation-model cost coefficients**, **not 2025 insurer sums insured**: require original building age/type/quality/estimated BGF, an independently justified 2010→2025 cost-index adjustment and explicit insured contents/BI/coverage and insurance-sum assumptions. No per-risk monetary TIV is approved from this source alone.

## Agriculture and commercial property — do not assume all R+V property is housing

A particularly strong source for **rural property opportunity**, now found in original open dataset form, is [**THÜNEN AGRARATLAS (2022), Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.14197441**](https://zenodo.org/records/14197441): it exposes three files **`animals_heads.txt`**, **`animals_lu.txt`**, **`crops.txt`** (approx **66 MB total**), years **1999/2003/2007/2010/2016/2020**, and **estimated municipality-level** animal/crop activity using **2010-epoch municipality keys**, including cattle, poultry and other stocking/land-use categories. **Crucial:** this provider explicitly warns it has *not been independently validated against observed municipality values*. It is not a census of physical stables or company insured addresses. Individual source licence (not clearly visible in search card), original bytes, 2010→2025 AGS boundaries, species codes and missing-value meaning still need review. Compare [official Destatis 5–10km Agrarstatistik](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Service/Statistik-Visualisiert/atlas-agrarstatistik.html) as partially correlated independent product, not double-counted farm events.

**SME and industrial property opportunity** should use the official [regional **`52111-01-02-4` table** for business establishments by district and employee-size group, 2019–2024](https://www.regionalstatistik.de/genesis/online?bypass=true&code=52111-01-02-4&levelid=1648536769031&levelindex=0&operation=table) with exact size-class definitions, official municipality/district boundary dates and suppressed cell handling. The [Statistikportal open-data policy](https://www.statistikportal.de/de/open-data) advertises Deutschland Namensnennung 2.0 reuse, but verify individual export and schema. **Employees and business branches are not property buildings, addresses or TIV.** Do not add rural farming facility exposures to the R+V Allgemeine **1,060,546 connected-building policies** as if those were all distinct commercial/residential addresses.

## Company entity guard: agricultural and trucking affiliates

The [R+V 2025 annual report portal](https://www.ruv.de/ueber-uns/unternehmen/geschaeftsberichte) includes **Vereinigte Tierversicherung Gesellschaft a.G.** as a **different** legal insurer with animal insurance lines. The [KRAVAG 2025 annual report portal](https://www.kravag.de/ueber-uns/unternehmen/geschaeftsberichte) separately covers KRAVAG-ALLGEMEINE, -LOGISTIC and -SACH, often motor and transport driven. Their data can constrain a **different** group-wide synthetic project but must never be silently added to **R+V Allgemeine solo** residential buildings or property premium/loss denominators.

## Experiment design now feasible after admission

Start with an **R+V-like Germany 2025 synthetic book with *separate* asset universes and geography priors**, all anchored only to proper publisher financial/contract population: (**A**) connected residential buildings 2025 1,060,546 issuer *contracts*, geography alternatives from 2022 Zensus; (**B**) separate SME buildings from official 2019–2024 district businesses; (**C**) rural farm/stable properties from independent census/estimated farmer proxies; (**D**) rooftop installed PV units from MaStR with separate NRW roof potential. A policy may span multiple buildings and outbuildings; no synthetic site = 1 observed company contract assertion.

Use **independent value**: local BGF/roof-class archetypes + NHK2010 adjusted to 2025; **scenario** policy deductibles, limits, flood optional endorsements (actual insurer cover fraction unknown); optional separate PV/machinery/BI. Produce 3 geographic hypotheses for each class (physical stock, building/occupation/value, channel/hazard-conditional) with preserved aggregate insurer year-end policies/premiums, and report sensitivity ranges instead of a precise R+V postcode portfolio.

Build event-replay *holdout* sets from **2021 Volker/Xero/Wolfgang/Bernd**, **2024 separate May-Saarland and June-Bavaria/BW flood**, and **2023 Benediktbeuern + 2024 Frieda hail**; preserve insurer solo vs group press distinctions. The **2021 R+V Allgemeine SOLO €949.8m gross annual elemental claim expense** remains an **annual gross** multievent challenge, NOT a single 2021 Bernd loss. Native GUL → IL → explicitly hypothetical ceded/net with identity-preserved event+period+sample across wind/flood/hail; calculate AAL/OEP/AEP only after a properly versioned, licensed Germany event catalogue and vulnerability validated against unrelated evidence.

**Do not claim current source register alone means geocoded R+V portfolio, installed assets insured, risk-location-level TIV, verified historical event water heights, current R+V reinsurance layer amounts, original bytes/license admission, or a calibrated Oasis/NextGen/Gross-Net OEP-AEP result.**
